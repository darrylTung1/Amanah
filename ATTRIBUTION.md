# Attribution

## Singapore map

The Singapore silhouette uses [geoBoundaries gbOpen SGP ADM0](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/SGP/ADM0/), boundary ID `SGP-ADM0-21272760`. The source represents a 2016 Urban Redevelopment Authority boundary derived from ADM3 and is distributed under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

The geographic data is stored in [`public/maps/singapore.geojson`](public/maps/singapore.geojson). Coordinates were projected, rounded, and styled as an illustrative silhouette. District markers are approximate and are not surveyed boundaries. The original download and additional details are recorded in [`public/maps/ATTRIBUTION.txt`](public/maps/ATTRIBUTION.txt).
