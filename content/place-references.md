# Place selection

The board is a conceptual district model. Its fictional conversations and policy effects are not claims about real operators, their leases or their plans. Religious-landmark scenarios concern surrounding public space and nearby premises, not ownership or reuse of the religious institution.

Chinatown uses Maxwell Food Centre, Chinatown Complex, Chinatown Heritage Centre, Kreta Ayer Heritage Gallery, Thian Hock Keng, Ann Siang Hill, Pagoda Street and Smith Street. Ann Siang Hill remains the greenery/access stop; Maxwell has its own hawker-livelihood scenario.

Kampong Glam uses Sultan Mosque, Malay Heritage Centre, Aliwal Arts Centre, Haji Lane, Arab Street, Bussorah Street, Kandahar Street and North Bridge Road. Named streets retain the living shops, food and cultural practice that complement the landmark institutions.
